This browser can't run the additive engine (it needs AudioWorklet). Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Flip the power switch, then play with Z–M (lower octave) and Q–P (upper octave), sharps on the row above each, or click the keys. Hold Space as a sustain pedal. A MIDI keyboard (notes, pedal, pitch wheel) works too in Chrome and Edge once the power is on. Every sound is a sum of up to 256 sine-wave harmonics: the screen shows them, and the filter curve shaping them.
